We were founded on the belief that freedom works. The freedom to work when and where you want, with who you want; the freedom to suit yourself, and the freedom to be yourself.

Together, we believe we can show the world that when people are free to work in the manner they choose, in an environment that encourages, supports, and celebrates them, the businesses they create together can become powerful platforms for change.

At Freedom Works, inclusivity is a reflex, not an initiative. For us, diversity is a business imperative as well as a moral obligation: a catalyst for creativity and innovation.

Our culture is built on equality, because we understand that the very best teams are made up of people who feel empowered to bring their whole selves to work.

Members of The Guild are trusted, respected, valued, and supported.
