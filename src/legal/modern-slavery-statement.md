Freedom Works is committed to good corporate governance and ethical business practices. This statement sets out Freedom Works’ approach to protecting against slavery and human trafficking in its business and supply chain, and is made in accordance with section 54 of the Modern Slavery Act 2015.

# Our supply chain

The suppliers we work with include those that directly support the services we provide to clients, such as production, research, and media agencies. They also include suppliers that support our business operations, such as professional services (including legal, tax, banking, and insurance).

# Our compliance steps

Since the introduction of the Act, we have put structures in place to ensure both our own and our suppliers’ compliance with it, including:

- assessing our supply chain for the risk of slavery or human trafficking
- complying with all of our employment law and regulatory obligations in the UK, with a process by which whistle-blowers can raise concerns in a confidential and protected environment, and a commitment to investigate allegations of wrongdoing

# Risk assessment

Taking into account the type of business we conduct and the type of suppliers we work with, we have assessed our exposure to slavery and human trafficking as very low risk, both within our own business and within our supply chain.

Where we identify potential risk within our supply chain, we will carry out due diligence on the suppliers concerned as part of our procurement process, to mitigate the risk.

# Supplier adherence to our values

We expect all of our business partners to meet the high standards we set for ourselves, and to ensure that their own organisations and supply chains are free from slavery and human trafficking.

We will monitor any risk areas, and we encourage anyone to report concerns about supplier activities.

# Training

To ensure a high level of understanding of the risks of modern slavery and human trafficking in our supply chains and our business, we provide our employees and workers with a copy of this statement, and encourage them to report any concerns to their manager.

# Our effectiveness in combating slavery and human trafficking

We use the following key performance indicators to measure how effective we have been in ensuring that slavery and human trafficking are not taking place in any part of our business or supply chains:

- information provided by preferred suppliers
- any concerns raised by our employees or workers

# Review

We will continue to review our approach to supply chain due diligence regularly, to ensure we have adequate structures, policies, and training in place to monitor and mitigate the risk of slavery and human trafficking in connection with our business. Any updates will be reflected in future statements.
