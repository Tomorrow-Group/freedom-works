The conversation explores something we believe deeply at The Liberty Guild: that the future of work isn’t about preserving old structures, it’s about building better ones.

Jon discusses with Tom May what he thinks about the Governments plan to appoint a Creative Freelance Champion, why freelancers need greater representation, why traditional agency models are struggling to support diverse talent, and why businesses should be designed around exceptional people rather than outdated systems.
