#### Every year, 200 km north of the Arctic Circle in a tiny village called Jukkasjärvi, they rebuild a beautiful hotel made entirely of ice. Every year it is bigger and better and more elaborate. And every spring it melts away into the Torne river that loaned them the water in the first place. They call it the circle of ice.

As marketeers, we have our own circle. And it’s this time of year when we start to rebuild our own halls of ice. Each year more ambitious and urgent, but with fewer hands to help and less resources.
