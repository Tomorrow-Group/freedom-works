Every summer, the world slows down just enough for you to read something that isn’t a brief.

We call ours *Beach Reads*. Bite-sized thinking for marketers who want ideas that travel further than their holiday luggage.

These aren’t “Top tips” posts. They’re thought starters. Provocations. Sometimes uncomfortable truths about the industry we love (and love to challenge).

Whether you’re reading poolside, between calls, or just stealing a quiet coffee, these pieces are here to make you think.

Over the next few weeks, we’ll share the *Beach Reads* series here on Substack, where our thinking goes straight to your inbox . No algorithm, no noise.

So pull up a sun lounger...
