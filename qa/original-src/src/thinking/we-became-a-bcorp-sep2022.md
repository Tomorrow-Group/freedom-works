The BCorp certification is the highest award in the world for how businesses treat their people, community and planet, through to transparency, performance and accountability. Our commitment goes all the way down to our Articles at Companies House. So you could say we’re an ethical supplier - a bit like Fairtrade is for coffee. And we proudly stand shoulder to shoulder with the likes of Ben and Jerrys, Patagonia and Toms, using our business as a force for good in the world. A ray of light in a world that can seem pretty grey at the moment.

We’re more than happy to chat to anyone who wants to know more about the BCorp journey to certification. Or how The Liberty Guild can help you creatively. (We won the Grand Prix for how we do that too…)
