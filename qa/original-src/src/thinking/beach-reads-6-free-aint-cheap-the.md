Well done. The emails are (mostly) ignored. The out-of-office is on. But your brain hasn’t quite powered down yet, has it?

That’s where *Beach Reads* comes in. Eight sun-drenched dispatches for marketers who secretly like a bit of mental stimulation with their SPF 50.

Here’s 6/8. A classic heist thriller.

This is all about theft, dilution, inflation; of attention, of value, of meaning. In this stylish caper: a marketing team tries to steal back value in a world where content is cheap but loyalty costs everything.

The client was sweating in linen.

“Someone’s stealing our value,” she whispered, sipping a €14 spritz.

I nodded. The briefcase was already open.

Charts. Screenshots. An avalanche of free guides, free content, free everything.

“Leads are drying up,” she said. “Our stuff used to *mean* something.”

I scanned the scene.

Every agency had a Substack now.

Every platform was a thought leader.

Generative AI was pumping out copy like a broken slot machine.

What used to be IP was now *RT’d and repackaged* before lunch.

We had a suspect.

His name was “FREE.”

He wore a smile and carried a zip file.

We went hunting.

The first clue was a lead magnet.

“Ultimate Guide to B2B Success.”

Twenty pages of ChatGPT sludge and unsourced stats.

I asked the founder what it had cost.

“Just a few hours and a Canva license.”

Then why wasn’t anyone converting?

He looked confused.

“Because everyone’s giving away the same thing.”

Bingo.

The problem wasn’t generosity.

It was sameness.

Devaluation by duplication.

We needed a new score.

Something *unstealable*.

Enter: The Scarcity Play.

Original research.

Voice-driven writing.

Live sessions with people who bleed when they speak.

Not scalable. Not replicable.

*Valuable.*

We ran the heist.

Swapped static PDFs for dynamic tools.

Traded content calendars for creator partnerships.

We made something only *they* could make.

Something that actually said something.

Two weeks later, the spritz tasted like victory.

People don’t want free.

They want **true**.

They want **proof**.

They want to feel like they just found something *no one else has*.

That’s value in 2025.

Everything else is just a downloadable nothing burger.
