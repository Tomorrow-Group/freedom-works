A known face does a lot of work upfront. Recognition, credibility, cultural shorthand. And when tolerance for failure drops, brands reach for things that reduce the number of unknowns.

It makes sense. But there's a long-term risk. If everyone consolidates around the same playbook big name, big spend, fewer moments. It gets very samey, very quickly. And that’s the irony. Safe starts to look risky in a different way.  
  
So, when everyone is playing it safe, where does distinctiveness really come from?
