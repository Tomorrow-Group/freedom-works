A pitch can be a powerful tool for showcasing creativity and building great partnerships, but let’s be honest, it’s also often unnecessarily complex, costly, and draining for everyone involved.

It doesn’t have to be this way. That’s why we’re signed up to the hashtag \#PitchPositivePledge to show our commitment to making pitching a more positive experience, for people, for profit, and for the planet. Less waste, more impact, and a more thoughtful approach to how we work together.

You can sign the pledge as well: <https://www.pitchpositivepledge.co.uk/>
