Fellow B Corp Coutts are using their premises in London to display the logos of over 240 female led B Corp businesses in the UK. So it is exciting to see The Liberty Guild there in all its glory.

And our championing of female talent goes beyond our day to day leadership. Of our nearly 400 Creative leaders, we’re proud to say that 33% are female. And we will continue to work at pace towards gender parity, and show the rest of the industry it can be done. (Globally creative leadership roles held by women are between 7 and 17%).

So, here’s to all the women we work with. Our partners, our members and our clients. Thank you.
